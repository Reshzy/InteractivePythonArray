## Direction contract

THESIS: `/` is the list stage. Refuse a marketing hero, demo strip, and scroll-to-tool landing.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream `#fffaf6`, near-black `#171717`, orange `#ff6b00` only on Run, selection, and changed cells. Geist Sans UI, Geist Mono for code and values. 12–16px radii, 1px warm border `#e4d8cc`, offset+blur shadow. No nested cards, no kicker labels.
